# Public outcome variables for celebrity–brand advertising and implicit product appearances

**Status:** sources and access conditions checked November 4, 2026. “Public” below means observable without being the focal advertiser, but some official APIs require an application, paid credits, researcher approval, or an affiliate/developer account. It does **not** mean unrestricted scraping.

## Measurement interpretation

1. **Opportunity-to-see (OTS) is not exposure.** An ad-library impression range, post view, news article, search result, or feed position indicates that content was delivered or available. It usually does not establish that a particular person noticed, processed, or remembered the celebrity–brand pairing.
2. **Most public measures are attention or association outcomes.** Search, pageviews, comments, product-identification posts, marketplace listings, and press volume can show that attention changed after an appearance. They do not by themselves show that the appearance caused the change.
3. **Use flows, not cumulative stocks.** Likes, views, ratings, installs, listings, and follower counts are usually cumulative/current snapshots. Archive them at a fixed cadence and analyze increments. Keep the raw snapshot timestamp.
4. **Pre-register directional queries.** Separate celebrity-only, brand-only, joint celebrity×brand, product/model, and competitor queries. This reduces post-treatment keyword selection and permits substitution/interference estimands.
5. **Best feasible design:** exact appearance/campaign time; unaffected comparison brands, celebrities, geographies, or products; high-frequency pre-period; event-study or synthetic-control model; placebo dates; exclusion of simultaneous launches, scandals, promotions, and paid media. Geographic randomized rollout, platform holdouts, or randomized ad delivery is much stronger when available.

### Causal-plausibility scale used below

- **Higher:** the variable can support a credible causal design when treatment timing or geography is plausibly exogenous and appropriate controls exist.
- **Moderate:** useful in an event study/DiD, but platform algorithms, other media, or demand-driven treatment create serious endogeneity.
- **Low:** primarily descriptive, a treatment/intensity measure, or too selected/aggregated to identify effectiveness alone.

## Recommended minimum outcome stack

For a defensible celebrity–brand study, collect at least one measure from each layer:

1. **Treatment/OTS:** campaign dates, ad-library creative-days and impression/reach bounds; public-post publication times.
2. **Attention:** Google Trends + Wikipedia pageviews.
3. **Interpretation/association:** joint mentions and comments from YouTube, Reddit, X, TikTok or Meta; explicitly code whether users inferred the correct brand/product.
4. **Behavioral proxy:** brand-site visits, app rank/downloads, affiliate-link creation, product-identification time, marketplace sold/listing activity, or price/availability.
5. **Business corroboration:** first-party sales/conversions where available; otherwise SEC segment sales or financial-market event response, clearly labeled as coarse/indirect.
6. **Interference controls:** competitor search share, concurrent competitor ads, competitor social/news volume, substitutes’ marketplace prices, and category seasonality.

### Priority estimands

- **Directional association:** change in the probability that a user-generated item mentioning the celebrity also names the focal brand, minus the analogous change for competitor brands.
- **Attention lift:** post-event change in focal-brand searches/pageviews relative to a synthetic control and to celebrity-only attention.
- **Competitive substitution:** change in focal brand’s share of total category search, conversation, site traffic, marketplace activity, or app rank—not just its own level.
- **Interference-adjusted effect:** focal outcome lift as a function of focal campaign intensity while controlling for competitor ad intensity and overlapping celebrity campaigns.
- **Product-identification efficiency:** time-to-correct-identification and probability of exact SKU identification after an implicit appearance.
- **Commercialization response:** new affiliate/product links, active/sold listings, price premium, stockout probability, app acquisition, or first-party conversion per unit of campaign delivery.
