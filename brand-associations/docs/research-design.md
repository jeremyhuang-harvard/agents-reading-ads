## 1. Mechanism or theory

Ads and appearances operate as associative cues. Shared category cues may retrieve competitors and create interference. Distinctive celebrity or cultural cues may instead create a relatively exclusive, directional association from the person or topic to the focal brand. The proposed asymmetry is that `celebrity → brand` can be strong while `brand → celebrity` remains weak.

## 2. Treatment or exposure

The long-run contrast is between generic category-focused advertising and advertising or appearances built around distinctive people or topics. The project must keep separate: an asset existing, a person being eligible, the asset being served, viewability, attention, noticing, identification, and later memory.

## 3. Measurement instrument

The proposed data foundation contains an appearance ledger, relationship ledger, amplification graph, and—only where available—viewer exposure or response data. Directed retrieval must be measured separately in both directions; symmetric embedding distance is insufficient by itself.

## 4. Outcomes and unit of observation

Candidate units include the media appearance, celebrity–brand–date relationship episode, amplification event, campaign creative, and person-level experimental response. Candidate outcomes include focal-brand retrieval, competitor retrieval, category retrieval, association directionality, memory, attribution, choice, and willingness to pay.

## 5. Identifying variation

The observational corpus is descriptive unless stronger variation is found. The preferred causal design is a matched-stimulus experiment varying the cue while holding the advertised product and other creative features fixed. Public-data designs and experimental designs should be developed separately.

## Distinctive empirical signature

The focal mechanism predicts that a distinctive celebrity or topic increases retrieval of the focal brand from that cue without proportionately increasing category or rival retrieval, while the reverse brand-to-celebrity association may remain weak. Generic category executions should generate more competitor retrieval than matched distinctive-cue executions.

## Open design gates

- select a bounded first market and corpus;
- choose declared campaigns, implicit appearances, or a matched sample;
- define stable entities, observation units, and directed-association measures;
- verify lawful and reproducible source access;
- specify human validation and a small pilot;
- preserve an experimental fallback if observational exposure or outcomes are unavailable.
