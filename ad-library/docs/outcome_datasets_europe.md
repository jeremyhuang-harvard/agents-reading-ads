# Feasible outcome datasets for advertising effects on gambling, betting, and prediction-market use in Europe

**Research date:** 8 October 2026  
**Interpretation:** “Losses” below generally means operator GGR/GGY/PBJ/BSR—stakes or deposits less winnings—not accounting profit. Operator accounts, regulator totals, bank debits, web visits, and app users measure different margins and should not be pooled without harmonization.

## Bottom line

1. **Best public causal setting: Spain.** DGOJ quarterly reports cover stakes, GGR, deposits, withdrawals, active/new accounts and operator marketing expenditure from the regulated online market’s 2012 start. Royal Decree 958/2020 introduced large, staged advertising restrictions in 2020–21; several digital/promotion provisions were annulled in May 2024, creating a partial reversal. This is unusually close to a policy–exposure–outcome panel, although all outcome data are national aggregates and the reforms overlap COVID and sports-calendar shocks.
2. **Best public high-frequency outcomes: Denmark.** The Danish Gambling Authority supplies downloadable monthly Excel data from January 2012, by gambling vertical, including GGR (player losses). It is ideal as a comparison series or for Danish events, but not operator-level.
3. **Best public long historical series: Great Britain.** UK Gambling Commission Excel data go back to FY2008/09 and are now quarterly by sector; a separate large-operator series provides monthly online GGY, bets/spins, active accounts and session metrics from March 2020. The latter begins too late for the August 2019 “whistle-to-whistle” advertising change and is contaminated by COVID-era disruption.
4. **Best subnational outcome: Italy’s public municipal physical-gambling files (2016–19).** These provide spend/player loss and tax by municipality; however the public page stops at 2019. ADM’s restricted SMART system has more recent territorial data (last three completed years plus current year, updated bimonthly) for authorized ADM/municipal officials, making an institutional data agreement potentially valuable.
5. **Best platform-level microstructure outcome:** Betfair historical exchange files (time-stamped market prices, settlement and traded volume) or public Polymarket wallet/trade APIs. Neither identifies users’ European location; geographic advertising effects therefore require a platform partnership, inferred location, or country-specific web/app traffic.
6. **Bank transactions are feasible only through partnership/procurement.** Published UK precedents demonstrate individual-day transaction outcomes at scale, but the underlying Lloyds/HSBC/YouGov Finance data are not public. A bank, open-banking aggregator, or regulator agreement is required.
7. If gambling use is inaccessible, **alcohol/tobacco administrative outcomes** offer stronger public high-frequency substitutes: Norway quarterly alcohol litres from 2002Q1; UK monthly alcohol/tobacco duty clearances; EU annual cigarette releases by country; and Eurostat monthly retail indices.

## F. Recommended empirical packages

### Package 1 — Public-data design (highest feasibility)
- **Treatment:** Spain 2020–21 restrictions and May 2024 partial reversal.
- **Primary outcomes:** DGOJ quarterly GGR, stakes, deposits, active/new accounts, marketing expenditure by vertical.
- **Mechanism/proxy:** Google Trends topics and exact brand terms by autonomous community; Similarweb country-domain visits if funded.
- **Controls:** Denmark monthly GGR, Sweden quarterly online GGR, France online PBJ/stakes; include sport-calendar fixed effects and COVID restrictions.
- **Design:** segmented event study/synthetic control; test betting vs casino/poker and advertising vs bonus/affiliate expenditure separately. Do not use a single “post” dummy because implementation was staged.

### Package 2 — Subnational exposure design
- **Treatment:** Italy 2018/19 ban or Spain ad restrictions.
- **Outcome:** obtain ADM SMART municipal/bimonthly physical gambling data, or commercial country/subregion web/app data; for Spain, Google Trends at autonomous-community level.
- **Exposure:** pre-policy local team sponsorship, televised-sport interest, betting-shop density, broadband/mobile adoption.
- **Design:** continuous-treatment difference-in-differences rather than treated-country vs untreated-country only.
- **Go/no-go:** do not proceed on Italy until a usable post-2019 ADM outcome series is contractually secured.

### Package 3 — Platform microstructure + geography proxy
- **Treatment:** country advertising policy or major campaign.
- **Outcome:** Betfair/Smarkets market-level matched volume and Polymarket trades; country-specific Similarweb/app/Google Trends as geographic shares.
- **Design:** event-market panel with country exposure interacted with market popularity; aggregate only markets/products legally available in each country.
- **Caveat:** platform APIs alone cannot identify European users, so claims must be about platform activity unless a defensible geographic allocation is added.

### Package 4 — Bank/open-banking partnership
- **Ask for:** stable pseudonymous person ID; transaction date/time; amount/direction; merchant/operator; country/region; all linked accounts; gambling deposits and withdrawals separately; cash/crypto flags; demographics; at least 24 months pre and post.
- **Minimum validation:** manually audit merchant mapping; distinguish lottery, sportsbook, casino, exchange and payment intermediary; measure panel entry/exit; test whether winnings return to the same bank; pre-register net-flow versus gross-deposit outcomes.
- **Likely partners:** UKGC procurement/research program, YouGov Finance, a large retail bank, Nordic administrative data holders, or an open-banking aggregator.

## Data-definition warnings

- **Stakes/turnover** repeatedly count recycled winnings; they can be 9–20+ times GGR depending product. Use GGR/player loss when studying economic harm, and stakes/bets when studying activity intensity.
- **Bank gambling debits** are deposits, not wagers or losses; withdrawals must be netted where possible, but cross-account/crypto/cash movement remains.
- **Active accounts** are not unique people within or across operators unless the regulator performs linkage.
- **Web visits/app MAU/search interest** are useful mechanisms or leading indicators, not monetary use.
- **Advertising bans change channel mix.** Measure TV, sponsorship, affiliates, bonuses, social/search and direct traffic separately where possible.
- **Legal-channel data can fall while total gambling does not** if users substitute to offshore operators; include channelisation/illegal-site traffic when available.
